# Vision Prompts

The prompts below were crafted based on the transcript of an interview from the **Close Encounter Club** YouTube channel, in which a witness describes in detail what he sees when looking into a diffracted red laser. The description is preserved here with full respect for the Code of Reality community and the original testimony.

**Source interview:** [https://youtu.be/I4JGLyrV7cQ](https://youtu.be/I4JGLyrV7cQ)

---

## Prompt 1

A vast cavernous interior glimpsed through a narrow crack in reality, built entirely from stacked, independently drifting layers of red light, like looking through a thousand panes of glass each holding a different image.

Nearest layer: a raw diffracted red laser beam split into three thin parallel lines, with faint radiating threads of light bleeding between them — textured like a nearly-dry paintbrush stroke.

Behind it: a churning back wall made of static, like television snow, composed of countless individual red pixels, each with its own faint shape, drifting and cascading rather than falling in a straight line.

In front of that wall, hovering mid-space: a semi-transparent membrane, like a soap bubble but without any rainbow iridescence, subtly warping whatever sits behind it. Faint silhouette-shapes surface within this membrane and fade away. Warm orange light glows through from behind it.

Further back: an ocean of gently rounded, organic island-like landmasses floating on a wavy, rippling sea of descending pixels, seen from a steep bird's-eye angle, like drone footage over an archipelago.

Deepest layer: massive architecture rendered only as glowing hollow outlines, never solid — twin lines converging to a peak with an empty gap in the center, like an obelisk with no interior; a front row of pyramid shapes of near-identical height lined up like teeth, more rows fading into hazy distance; jagged sawtooth ridges far in the background; a long cylindrical form half-buried in a drifting field of pixels, particles streaming off its sides; curved hook-like forms suggestive of wings, curling outward then snapping sharply backward.

Zooming into any surface reveals it is built from swarms of tiny glowing glyphs resembling CodeOfReality characters, densely packed and receding infinitely — one visible cluster forms a radiating star shape from crossing lines.

Atmosphere: the viewer feels impossibly small, as if peering through a narrow tunnel opening into an enormous underground hall. Looking straight up reveals a ceiling that never resolves, only brightening as it recedes into depth. The bottom edges of the whole field slant subtly inward while the top stays flat and perfectly uniform. Strong independent parallax between all layers. Everything feels deliberately engineered and constructed rather than organic. The color palette is dominated entirely by deep saturated red, broken only by the warm orange glow bleeding through the translucent membrane. The closer one looks, the more vertiginous it becomes — a sense of standing at the edge of a balcony, on the verge of falling deeper into the structure.

Volumetric lighting, extreme depth of field with independently moving layers, hyper-detailed fractal micro-patterns, dark void background, glitch-like instability at the edges of focus, cinematic sci-fi concept art, ultra-detailed.

## Prompt 2

A vast, infinite dimensional cavern glimpsed through a narrow opening in reality, as if looking through dozens of stacked transparent glass panes, each holding a different layer of the scene. Countless microscopic red pixels cascade endlessly downward like static on an old CRT television, forming a shimmering curtain of falling light-code. A translucent membrane hovers between layers — like a soap bubble without its rainbow sheen — subtly warping the massive structures glowing behind it: sharp obelisk shapes formed by two converging lines meeting at a hollow point, a long row of pyramid-like silhouettes lined up like teeth stretching into unfathomable depth, jagged sawtooth ridges, and curved wing-like forms hooking sharply backward. In the middle distance, smooth organic island shapes float on a rippling, wavy sea of falling particles, seen from a bird's-eye drone-like angle. Zooming closer, every surface dissolves into dense fields of tiny glowing characters resembling CodeOfReality glyphs, embedded within the falling pixel-rain. The scene glows mostly deep red, with warm orange light bleeding through the translucent membrane. Everything feels colossal, precise, and deliberately engineered rather than organic — an architecture of pure information extending infinitely in every direction, including depth. Volumetric red glow, deep parallax layers, hyper-detailed fractal micro-patterns, dark void background, vertiginous sense of scale, digital-organic hybrid aesthetic, cinematic sci-fi concept art.
